What we do

Child & family-centred care

SickKids provides health care with a focus on the unique needs of children and their families. Family Spaces provide areas for recreation and activities to enhance patient and family experience, while Interpreter Services and Spiritual & Religious Care help ensure that care is inclusive and reponsive to the diverse needs of every family.

Innovation in child health research

The SickKids Research Institude is Canada's largest, hospital-based child health research institute. It is dedicated to improving the health and well-being of children through research. Our research programs range from basic discovery all the way to clincal care. These programs are supported by state-of-the-art expertise, technologies, and facilities.

SickKids is one of Canada's top 40 research hospitals.

Teaching the next generation of health-care professionals

The SickKids Learning Institute (LI) works to improve child health outcomes health-care systems through education and training. To support faculty development, educate parents and families, and inspire learners from all around the world, we have developed collaborative and innovative partnerships and programming.

Worldwide activities

SickKids engages in a wide range of intiatitives to improve child health all around the world. These include the SickKids Centre for Global Child Health, SickKids International, our International Patient Program, and more.
