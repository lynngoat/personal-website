What we do

The Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids), in partnership with the University of Toronto, is Canada's leading pediatric hospital and one of the country's most research intensive healthcare institutions.

We are dedicated exclusively to children's health. SickKids has built a reputation for delivering the best care while driving innovation in research, education, and patient treatment.

More than just a hospital, SickKids is a place where compassionate, family-centred care is combined with groundbreaking discoveries to create better outcomes for both patients today and in the future.
